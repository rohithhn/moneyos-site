Log it any way you like

  1. Say it.

    Speak “chai 200, petrol 1200, uber 340” once and get three entries. Understood on your iPhone. Your voice never goes to a server.

  2. Type it.

    One line, like a text message, is split into separate entries. Several at once open a review list you can edit before saving.

  3. Screenshot it.

    Pick up to 10 screenshots of your payment history from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or your bank app. Every row becomes a transaction, and likely repeats are flagged.

  4. Import or paste it.

    CSV and PDF bank statements, including password-protected PDFs. Or paste a bank SMS, or share a bank email from Mail. Nothing saves without your OK.

Why offline matters

Money apps usually want a login, then your bank credentials, then a subscription. MoneyOS wants none of those. Everything is stored on this iPhone. iCloud sync is optional, off by default, and goes through your own private iCloud, which we cannot see.

Data Not Collected

That’s our App Privacy label. No analytics, no crash reporting, no ad SDK, no tracking.

Face ID lock

Lock the app behind Face ID, so a borrowed phone doesn’t mean a borrowed ledger.

Backups that outlive the app

Encrypted daily backups, copied to a folder you pick in Files or iCloud Drive.

Your data, out any time

Export everything to CSV or JSON whenever you want it.

A money app should be a private notebook, not a data pipeline.
