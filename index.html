Adding money in takes seconds

  1. Screenshot your UPI history

    Take a screenshot in PhonePe, Google Pay or Paytm — pick several at once — and every row on each one is read into a review list.

  2. Type one line

    “chai 200, petrol 1200” fills the amount, merchant and category. Several at once open a review list instead.

  3. Say it

    Speak a few expenses in one go. Parsed on device — your voice never goes to a server.

  4. Statements and SMS

    Paste a bank SMS, or drop a PDF or CSV statement. You review every row before it saves.

Why offline matters

Money apps usually want a login, then your bank credentials, then a subscription. MoneyOS wants none of those. Your transactions, cards, income and goals live in one place: this iPhone. iCloud sync is the single exception, it is yours to switch on, and it syncs through your own private iCloud account — which we cannot see.
